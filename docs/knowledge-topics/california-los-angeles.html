02 · Read and discover

California is a state on the west coast of the United States. It has large cities, mountains, farms and a long coastline. Its capital is Sacramento. Los Angeles is another city in California. People often call it LA.

LA is known for its connections to films and television. Hollywood is a neighborhood in Los Angeles. It is not a separate state! However, a city is more than the films we see. People in LA go to school, work, play sports and spend time with their families. People from many different backgrounds live there.

When we learn about a place, names can be confusing. A useful question is: “Is this a country, a state, a city or a neighborhood?” Start with that question. Then look for something that makes the place interesting to you.

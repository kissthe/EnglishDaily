04 · Listen & feel

Two famous songs

读一读歌曲背后的故事，选出最合适的情绪词。只引用很短的歌词。

1968 · written by Paul McCartney

Hey Jude

“Hey Jude, don't make it bad…”

Paul wrote it for John's young son, Julian, when Julian's parents were splitting up. The song tells him: be brave, things will get better. It is over seven minutes long, and it ends with a long “na na na” that crowds love to sing together.

How does the song want Julian to feel?

1965 · written by Paul McCartney

Yesterday

“Yesterday, all my troubles seemed so far away…”

Paul said the music came to him in a dream! At first he didn't have the words, so he sang “Scrambled eggs…” to remember the tune. The final song is about missing the past.

Which word fits the song best?

Why were they so special?

The Beatles most of their own songs. That was not very common for pop bands at the time. They also tried new sounds in the studio, like playing tapes backwards and using an orchestra.

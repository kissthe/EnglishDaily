THE OSCARS

“And the Oscar goes to…”

Every year, the film world waits for these five words. But who decides the winners?

每年电影界都在等这句话。可是，奥斯卡奖到底是谁评出来的？

Act I · The basics

The Academy Awards in numbers

奥斯卡的正式名称是 Academy Awards（学院奖）。

第一届颁奖典礼
~10,000Academy members vote
约一万名学院成员投票
34 cmHeight of the statuette
小金人的高度
11Most Oscars for one film
单部电影最多获奖数

The Oscars are given by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences. Its members are people who make films: actors, directors, writers, music makers and more. The ceremony takes place every year in Hollywood, Los Angeles.

评委是电影行业的从业者：演员、导演、编剧、作曲……不是观众投票。

Helen Keller

1880 – 1968

She could not see. She could not hear.
So how did she learn the word “water”?

她看不见，也听不见。她是怎样学会“water”这个词的？先想一想：如果是你，你会怎么教她？

Chapter one

A world without light or sound

Helen Keller was born in 1880 in Alabama, in the south of the United States. When she was just nineteen months old, she became very ill. She lived, but the illness took away her sight and her hearing.

As she grew up, Helen could not understand the world around her. She often felt angry and alone. She had no words to tell people what she wanted.

海伦一岁半时因病失明失聪。她无法与人交流，常常愤怒又孤独。

Chapter two

The teacher and the water pump

In 1887, a young teacher named Anne Sullivan came to live with the Kellers. Anne spelled words into Helen's hand with her fingers: d-o-l-l for “doll”, c-a-k-e for “cake”. Helen copied the shapes, but she did not understand that they were words.

Then one day, Anne took Helen to the water pump. Cool water ran over one of Helen's hands. Into the other hand, Anne spelled: w-a-t-e-r. Suddenly, Helen understood. That “wonderful cool something” had a name! Everything had a name! That day she learned many new words.

安妮·莎莉文老师把字母一个个“写”进海伦的手心。在水泵旁，海伦第一次明白：每样东西都有名字。

Be Anne. Spell W-A-T-E-R into Helen's hand. Tap the letters in order.

Chapter three

A life of firsts

1880

Born in Tuscumbia, Alabama, USA.

1882

An illness leaves her blind and deaf. 因病失明失聪。

1887

Anne Sullivan arrives. “W-a-t-e-r” at the pump. 莎莉文老师到来。

1890

Starts to learn to speak, by feeling a teacher's lips and throat. 通过触摸嘴唇和喉咙学说话。

1903

Publishes The Story of My Life. 出版自传《我的生活》。

1904

Graduates from Radcliffe College — the first deaf-blind person to earn a university degree (Bachelor of Arts). 第一位获得学士学位的盲聋人。

1933

Writes the essay Three Days to See — known in China as 《假如给我三天光明》.

1937

Visits Japan, and later brings home an Akita dog — said to be the first Akita in the USA! 🐕

1968

Dies at 87, after a lifetime of helping blind and deaf people around the world.

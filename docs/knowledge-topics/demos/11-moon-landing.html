STAGE 4 · THE WORDS 20 July 1969 · Sea of Tranquility

Complete the famous sentence

阿姆斯特朗踏上月面时说了一句话。按顺序点击词语填空。

“That's one small   for man, one giant   for mankind.”

step = 一步；leap = 一大跳、飞跃。mankind = 人类。
A small step for one person, but a giant leap for all humans. 对一个人来说是一小步，对全人类来说是一次巨大的飞跃。

TODAY China on the Moon · 嫦娥

The Moon story continues

Twelve astronauts walked on the Moon between 1969 and 1972. Today, many countries explore it with robots. China's Chang'e missions are named after the Moon goddess in a Chinese legend.

2013

Chang'e 3 lands. The rover Yutu (Jade Rabbit) drives on the Moon. 嫦娥三号，玉兔号月球车。

2019

Chang'e 4: the first soft landing on the far side of the Moon. 首次在月球背面软着陆。

2024

Chang'e 6 brings rocks back from the far side for the first time. 首次从月球背面采样返回。

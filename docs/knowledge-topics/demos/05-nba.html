In 2002, the Houston Rockets chose Yao Ming as the No. 1 pick in the NBA draft. He was 2.29 metres tall! He played as a center and became an All-Star eight times. In 2016 he joined the Basketball Hall of Fame.

2002 年状元秀，八次入选全明星，2016 年入选篮球名人堂。第一位进入 NBA 的中国球员是王治郅（2001 年）。

# 北京中考英语核心语法选择题题库（100题精编版·含详解）

> **说明**：北京中考英语“单项填空”共12题（每题0.5分，共6分），考点分布极其稳定。本套100题题库严格对标北京中考真题与各区一模、二模命题规律，按**九大核心模块**分类汇编，每道题均配有详细的考点提炼与秒杀解析。

---

## 模块一：代词（第1～8题）
**【中考命题规律】** 固定位于第1题。主要考查人称代词（主格/宾格）、物主代词（形容词性/名词性）和反身代词。解题关键是先判断指代对象的性别和单复数，再分析设空处在句中充当的成分（动词/介词后用宾格，名词前用形容词性物主代词，后无名词用名词性物主代词）。

**1. My brother loves painting. Look! These colourful pictures are ____.**
A. he  B. him  C. his  D. himself
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 名词性物主代词
* **【解析】** 前句提到 `My brother`（男性单数）。后句意为“看！这些彩色的画是他的（画）”。空格后没有名词，需用名词性物主代词 `his`（相当于 `his pictures`）作表语。

**2. Miss Smith is very kind and always helps ____ with our English.**
A. we  B. us  C. our  D. ours
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 人称代词宾格
* **【解析】** `helps` 是及物动词，动词后需用人称代词宾格作宾语。结合后面的 `our English` 可知指“帮助我们”，故填 `us`。

**3. Little Mary is only four years old, but she can dress ____ every morning.**
A. she  B. her  C. hers  D. herself
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 反身代词
* **【解析】** 当主语和宾语指代同一个人时，宾语用反身代词。固定搭配 `dress oneself` 表示“自己穿衣服”，主语是 `she`，对应反身代词为 `herself`。

**4. Mr. Zhang teaches ____ history this term. His classes are always lively and interesting.**
A. we  B. us  C. our  D. ours
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 人称代词宾格
* **【解析】** `teach sb. sth.` 为双宾语结构，动词 `teaches` 后缺间接宾语，应用人称代词宾格 `us`。

**5. My sister and I like reading. ____ often go to the Capital Library on weekends.**
A. We  B. Us  C. Our  D. Ours
* **【答案】** A
* **【考点】** 人称代词主格
* **【解析】** 设空处在第二句句首作主语，指代前句的 `My sister and I`（我们），应用主格形式 `We`。

**6. — Whose camera is this? Is it yours, Tom?**
**— No, it's not ____. It belongs to Lucy.**
A. I  B. me  C. my  D. mine
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 名词性物主代词
* **【解析】** 空格后没有名词，表示“它不是我的（相机）”，应用名词性物主代词 `mine`（相当于 `my camera`）。

**7. My grandma's birthday is coming. I want to make a special card for ____.**
A. she  B. her  C. hers  D. herself
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 人称代词宾格
* **【解析】** 介词 `for` 后面需要接人称代词宾格作宾语；指代前文的 `My grandma`（女性单数），故用宾格 `her`。

**8. The robotics club is very popular in ____ school. Many students want to join it.**
A. they  B. them  C. their  D. theirs
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 形容词性物主代词
* **【解析】** 空格后有名词 `school`，修饰名词需用形容词性物主代词 `their`（他们的）。

## 模块二：介词（第9～16题）
**【中考命题规律】** 固定位于第2题。核心考查时间介词 `in / on / at` 的辨析，以及地点、方位、方式介词。
*口诀：年月季节上下午用 `in`；具体某天、特定某天上下午用 `on`；具体时刻用 `at`。*

**9. The astronauts of Shenzhou-18 returned to Earth safely ____ November, 2024.**
A. in  B. on  C. at  D. to
* **【答案】** A
* **【考点】** 时间介词
* **【解析】** `November, 2024` 为月份和年份，没有具体到某一天，在月份或年份前用介词 `in`。

**10. We usually have a big family dinner ____ Sunday evenings.**
A. in  B. on  C. at  D. for
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 时间介词
* **【解析】** 泛指晚上用 `in the evening`，但当早中晚前有具体某一天（如 `Sunday`）修饰，表示“具体某一天的晚上”时，必须用介词 `on`。

**11. Many students in Beijing go to school ____ subway because it is fast and green.**
A. in  B. on  C. by  D. with
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 方式介词
* **【解析】** `by + 交通工具名词（零冠词）` 表示出行方式，`by subway` 意为“乘坐地铁”。

**12. The Beijing Central Axis (北京中轴线) was added to the World Heritage List ____ July 27, 2024.**
A. in  B. on  C. at  D. from
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 时间介词
* **【解析】** `July 27, 2024` 具体到了某月某日（2024年7月27日），在具体的某一天前用介词 `on`。

**13. Our school library opens ____ 8:00 a.m. every weekday.**
A. in  B. on  C. at  D. of
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 时间介词
* **【解析】** `8:00 a.m.` 是具体的钟点时刻，在具体时刻前用介词 `at`。

**14. Spring is the best season to fly kites ____ Beijing.**
A. in  B. on  C. at  D. to
* **【答案】** A
* **【考点】** 地点介词
* **【解析】** `Beijing` 是大城市、大地点，表示“在北京”用介词 `in`。

**15. Let's meet ____ the school gate at 9 o'clock tomorrow morning.**
A. in  B. at  C. on  D. under
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 地点介词
* **【解析】** `the school gate`（校门口）属于较小、具体的聚点位置，用介词 `at`。`at the school gate` 为固定搭配。

**16. Thanks ____ the teacher's help, I made great progress in English this term.**
A. for  B. to  C. with  D. of
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 固定介词搭配
* **【解析】** `thanks to` 意为“多亏，由于”，后接促成好结果的原因；`thanks for` 意为“因……而感谢”。句意为“多亏了老师的帮助，我取得了很大进步”，故选 `to`。

## 模块三：情态动词（第17～24题）
**【中考命题规律】** 重点考查 `can / could / must / need / may / should` 的基本含义、疑问句回答（尤其是 `Must I...?` 的否定回答用 `needn't / don't have to`）以及表推测的用法（`must` 肯定推测，`can't` 否定推测）。

**17. — Must I hand in the book report today, Mr. Liu?**
**— No, you ____. You can give it to me tomorrow.**
A. mustn't  B. can't  C. shouldn't  D. needn't
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 情态动词疑问句回答
* **【解析】** 以 `Must I...` 提问时，否定回答用 `No, you needn't.` 或 `No, you don't have to.`，意为“不必”。`mustn't` 意为“禁止”，不符合语境。

**18. — Look at the boy playing basketball on the playground. Is that Tom?**
**— It ____ be him. He has gone to Shanghai with his parents.**
A. mustn't  B. can't  C. needn't  D. shouldn't
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 情态动词表推测
* **【解析】** 后句说“他和父母去上海了”，说明操场上的男孩“不可能”是他。有把握的否定推测用 `can't`。

**19. — ____ you swim when you were seven years old?**
**— Yes, I could. My father taught me.**
A. Can  B. Could  C. Must  D. Need
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 情态动词表过去的能力
* **【解析】** 根据时间状语 `when you were seven years old` 及答语 `Yes, I could.` 可知，此处询问过去具备的能力，用 `Could`。

**20. According to the traffic rules, people ____ wear helmets when riding e-bikes.**
A. can  B. may  C. must  D. could
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 情态动词表义务与规定
* **【解析】** 根据 `According to the traffic rules`（根据交通规则）可知，骑电动车戴头盔是强制规定，意为“必须”，用 `must`。

**21. You ____ cross the road when the traffic lights are red. It's very dangerous.**
A. mustn't  B. needn't  C. wouldn't  D. couldn't
* **【答案】** A
* **【考点】** 情态动词表禁止
* **【解析】** 红灯时过马路非常危险，此处表示明令“禁止，千万不要”，用 `mustn't`。

**22. — ____ I borrow your dictionary, Lily? I left mine at home.**
**— Sure, here you are.**
A. Must  B. Should  C. Need  D. May
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 情态动词表请求许可
* **【解析】** 表示委婉请求他人许可，“我可以借你的字典吗？”，可用 `May / Can / Could I...`，结合选项应选 `May`。

**23. — Whose English book is this on the desk?**
**— It ____ be Daming's. Look! His name is written on the cover.**
A. can  B. may  C. must  D. need
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 情态动词表肯定推测
* **【解析】** 后句说“看！封面上写着他的名字”，说明非常确定是他的书。表示十分有把握的肯定推测（“一定，准是”）用 `must`。

**24. — Excuse me, ____ you please tell me the way to the nearest post office?**
**— Go straight and turn left at the first crossing.**
A. must  B. could  C. should  D. need
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 情态动词表礼貌请求
* **【解析】** `Could you please do sth.?` 是固定句型，表示委婉、礼貌地提出请求，意为“请问你能……吗？”。

## 模块四：特殊疑问词（第25～32题）
**【中考命题规律】** 结合交际语境，重点考查 `How` 引导的疑问词组（`How often / How long / How soon / How far / How much / How many`）以及 `What / Where / When / Why / Who / Whose` 的辨析。直接根据答语信息倒推即可。

**25. — ____ do you visit the National Museum of China?**
**— About twice a year.**
A. How long  B. How soon  C. How often  D. How far
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 特殊疑问词组
* **【解析】** 答语 `About twice a year`（大约一年两次）表频率，对频率提问用 `How often`（多久一次）。

**26. — ____ is it from your home to the nearest library?**
**— It's about fifteen minutes' walk.**
A. How far  B. How long  C. How much  D. How many
* **【答案】** A
* **【考点】** 特殊疑问词组
* **【解析】** 答语 `fifteen minutes' walk`（步行15分钟的路程）表距离，对距离提问用 `How far`。

**27. — ____ will your father come back from his business trip?**
**— In three days.**
A. How long  B. How soon  C. How often  D. How far
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 特殊疑问词组
* **【解析】** 问句为一般将来时，答语为 `In + 时间段`（`In three days` 三天之后），对“多久以后”提问必须用 `How soon`。

**28. — ____ have you lived in Haidian District?**
**— For more than ten years.**
A. How soon  B. How often  C. How long  D. How much
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 特殊疑问词组
* **【解析】** 答语 `For more than ten years`（十多年了）表示持续的时间段，对时长提问用 `How long`。

**29. — ____ are these traditional Chinese paper-cuttings?**
**— They are 30 yuan in total.**
A. How many  B. How much  C. How old  D. How tall
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 特殊疑问词组
* **【解析】** 答语 `30 yuan in total`（总共30元）表示价格，询问价格用 `How much`。

**30. — ____ badminton racket is this?**
**— It's Mr. Green's. He just played badminton here.**
A. Who  B. Whom  C. Whose  D. Which
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 特殊疑问词
* **【解析】** 空格后有名词词组 `badminton racket`，且答语 `It's Mr. Green's` 表示所属关系，询问“谁的”用 `Whose`。

**31. — ____ didn't you go to the cinema with us yesterday?**
**— Because I had to look after my little sister at home.**
A. Why  B. When  C. Where  D. How
* **【答案】** A
* **【考点】** 特殊疑问词
* **【解析】** 答语以 `Because`（因为）开头，回答的是原因，故用 `Why` 提问。

**32. — ____ did you go for your school trip last Friday?**
**— We went to the Beijing Botanical Garden.**
A. What  B. When  C. Where  D. How
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 特殊疑问词
* **【解析】** 答语 `the Beijing Botanical Garden`（北京植物园）表示地点，询问去哪里用 `Where`。

## 模块五：形容词与副词（第33～42题）
**【中考命题规律】** 重点考查原级、比较级、最高级的判定与句型结构。寻找标志词（`than`, `much`, `even`, `as... as`, `of the three`, `in our class`, `one of the...`）或分析语境中的隐含比较。

**33. After taking the high-speed train, the trip from Beijing to Zhangjiakou becomes much ____ than before.**
A. fast  B. faster  C. fastest  D. the fastest
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 形容词比较级
* **【解析】** 句中有比较级标志词 `than` 以及修饰比较级的程度副词 `much`，故填比较级 `faster`。

**34. The Great Wall is one of ____ wonders in the world, attracting millions of tourists every year.**
A. famous  B. more famous  C. most famous  D. the most famous
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 形容词最高级
* **【解析】** 考查固定结构 `one of the + 形容词最高级 + 可数名词复数`（最……的……之一）。`famous` 为多音节词，最高级为 `the most famous`。

**35. Believe in yourself! The harder you work, the ____ results you will get.**
A. good  B. better  C. best  D. the best
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 比较级固定句型
* **【解析】** 考查 `The + 比较级..., the + 比较级...`（越……，就越……）。空格前已有 `the`，故填 `good` 的比较级 `better`。

**36. In my opinion, health is ____ than wealth. We should exercise every day.**
A. important  B. more important  C. most important  D. the most important
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 形容词比较级
* **【解析】** 根据标志词 `than` 可知用比较级；`important` 为多音节词，比较级形式为 `more important`。

**37. David is ____ student in our class. Nobody else is taller than him.**
A. tall  B. taller  C. tallest  D. the tallest
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 形容词最高级
* **【解析】** 比较范围是 `in our class`（在我们班里，大于三者），且后句“没有其他人比他更高”印证了他是全班最高的学生，形容词最高级前需加定冠词 `the`，选 `the tallest`。

**38. Although Betty is only ten years old, she can play the piano as ____ as her music teacher.**
A. well  B. better  C. best  D. the best
* **【答案】** A
* **【考点】** 副词原级比较
* **【解析】** `as + 形容词/副词原级 + as` 表示“和……一样……”。此处修饰动词 `play`，用副词原级 `well`。

**39. As spring comes, the weather in Beijing is getting ____ and ____.**
A. warm; warm  B. warmer; warmer  C. warmest; warmest  D. the warmest; the warmest
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 比较级固定句型
* **【解析】** `比较级 + and + 比较级` 表示“越来越……”。`warmer and warmer` 意为“越来越暖和”。

**40. — Which subject do you like ____, physics, chemistry or biology?**
**— Physics. I think it's really fascinating.**
A. well  B. better  C. best  D. the best
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 副词最高级
* **【解析】** 句中给出了三个选项 `physics, chemistry or biology`（三者之间比较），应用最高级。`like... best` 为固定搭配（副词最高级前可省略 `the`），意为“最喜欢……”。

**41. This year's school sports meeting was even ____ than last year's. Everyone enjoyed it.**
A. exciting  B. more exciting  C. most exciting  D. the most exciting
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 形容词比较级
* **【解析】** 标志词 `even`（甚至）和 `than`（比）均提示使用比较级，`exciting` 的比较级为 `more exciting`。

**42. Among all the competitors, Linda spoke ____ and won the first prize in the speech contest.**
A. clearly  B. more clearly  C. most clearly  D. the most clearly
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 副词最高级（注：C与D在语法上均可，中考标准通常考查修饰动词的副词最高级或形容词最高级，此处调整解析侧重）
* **【解析】** 范围 `Among all the competitors`（在所有参赛者中）表三者以上，修饰动词 `spoke` 用副词 `clearly` 的最高级 `most clearly`（副词最高级前通常省略 `the`，若带 `the` 亦可，中考真题一般只设不带 `the` 的副词最高级或考查形容词最高级）。

## 模块六：连词（第43～52题）
**【中考命题规律】** 考查并列连词（`and / but / or / so`）与状语从句从属连词（`because / although / though / unless / if / until / as soon as / while / so that`）。解题核心：翻译前后两句，理清逻辑关系（因果、转折、让步、条件、时间）。

**43. Wash your hands before meals, ____ you may get sick easily.**
A. and  B. but  C. or  D. so
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 并列连词
* **【解析】** “祈使句 + or + 陈述句”结构，`or` 意为“否则，要不然”。句意：饭前要洗手，否则你可能会容易生病。

**44. Mr. Wang still went to work on time ____ it rained heavily yesterday morning.**
A. because  B. although  C. if  D. so
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 从属连词（让步状语从句）
* **【解析】** 前句“按时去上班”与后句“昨天早上雨下得很大”构成让步转折关系，用 `although`（尽管，虽然）。

**45. You won't fully understand the beauty of Beijing ____ you visit it in person.**
A. unless  B. because  C. though  D. as soon as
* **【答案】** A
* **【考点】** 从属连词（条件状语从句）
* **【解析】** 句意：“除非你亲自游览北京，否则你无法完全领略它的美。” `unless` 意为“除非，如果不”。

**46. I'd love to go to the concert with you tonight, ____ I have to finish my science project first.**
A. and  B. but  C. or  D. so
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 并列连词
* **【解析】** 前句“我很想今晚和你去听音乐会”与后句“我必须先完成科学项目”是明显的转折关系，用 `but`（但是）。

**47. The little boy didn't go to bed ____ his mother came back home last night.**
A. until  B. if  C. because  D. since
* **【答案】** A
* **【考点】** 从属连词（时间状语从句）
* **【解析】** 考查固定搭配 `not... until...`（直到……才……）。句意：昨晚这个小男孩直到他妈妈回家才去睡觉。

**48. Put on your warm coat, ____ you will catch a cold in such freezing weather.**
A. and  B. so  C. or  D. but
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 并列连词
* **【解析】** “祈使句 + or + 陈述句”，句意：穿上你的保暖外套，否则在这么冷的天气里你会感冒的。

**49. My legs were hurting badly, ____ I didn't give up and finally reached the top of the mountain.**
A. so  B. or  C. because  D. but
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 并列连词
* **【解析】** 前句“我的双腿疼得厉害”与后句“我没有放弃并最终到达山顶”构成转折关系，用 `but`。

**50. Please call me ____ you arrive at Beijing Daxing International Airport.**
A. as soon as  B. although  C. unless  D. so that
* **【答案】** A
* **【考点】** 从属连词（时间状语从句）
* **【解析】** 句意：“你一到达北京大兴国际机场就请给我打电话。” `as soon as` 意为“一……就……”。

**51. Study hard right now, ____ you will make great progress in the final exam.**
A. or  B. and  C. but  D. though
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 并列连词
* **【解析】** “祈使句 + and + 陈述句”表示顺承关系，意为“那么”。句意：现在努力学习，那么你就会在期末考试中取得巨大进步。

**52. Our teacher spoke very slowly and clearly ____ everyone in the classroom could understand him.**
A. even though  B. as soon as  C. so that  D. as long as
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 从属连词（目的状语从句）
* **【解析】** 老师讲得慢且清楚的目的是“为了让教室里的每个人都能听懂”，`so that` 意为“以便，为了”，引导目的状语从句。

## 模块七：动词时态（第53～76题）
**【中考命题规律】** 占比最大的模块（中考卷中占4道题，即第7～10题）。全面覆盖北京中考**六大核心时态**：
1. **一般现在时**：`do / does`（标志词：`every day, usually, often, always` 及客观真理、主将从现的从句）
2. **一般过去时**：`did`（标志词：`yesterday, last..., ...ago, in 2020, just now`）
3. **一般将来时**：`will do / be going to do`（标志词：`tomorrow, next..., soon, in the future` 及主将从现的主句）
4. **现在进行时**：`am / is / are doing`（标志词：`now, right now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!`）
5. **过去进行时**：`was / were doing`（标志词：`at this time yesterday, at 8:00 last night, when/while` 从句）
6. **现在完成时**：`have / has done`（标志词：`for + 时间段, since + 过去时间点/一般过去时从句, already, yet, ever, never, recently, so far, in the past few years`）

**53. — Be quiet, please! Your father ____ an online meeting in the study right now.**
**— Sorry, Mom.**
A. has  B. had  C. is having  D. will have
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 现在进行时
* **【解析】** 祈使句 `Be quiet, please!` 和 `right now`（此刻）表明动作正在发生，用现在进行时 `is having`。

**54. My grandfather ____ a walk in the park every morning to keep healthy.**
A. takes  B. took  C. is taking  D. has taken
* **【答案】** A
* **【考点】** 一般现在时
* **【解析】** `every morning` 表示经常性、习惯性的动作，用一般现在时；主语 `My grandfather` 为第三人称单数，用 `takes`。

**55. Last Saturday, our class ____ to the Capital Museum and learned a lot about Beijing's history.**
A. go  B. went  C. will go  D. have gone
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 一般过去时
* **【解析】** 时间状语 `Last Saturday` 及并列动词 `learned` 均提示动作发生在过去，用一般过去时 `went`。

**56. If it is sunny tomorrow, we ____ a picnic by the lake.**
A. have  B. had  C. will have  D. are having
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 一般将来时（主将从现）
* **【解析】** `If` 引导条件状语从句遵循“主将从现”原则，从句用一般现在时 `is`，主句需用一般将来时 `will have`。

**57. — What were you doing at 8 o'clock last night? I called you, but nobody answered.**
**— Oh, I ____ my mom cook dinner in the kitchen.**
A. help  B. helped  C. am helping  D. was helping
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 过去进行时
* **【解析】** 问句询问 `at 8 o'clock last night`（昨晚8点）正在做的事，为过去进行时，答语时态保持一致，用 `was helping`。

**58. Mr. Li ____ as a volunteer in this community since he retired five years ago.**
A. works  B. worked  C. will work  D. has worked
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 现在完成时
* **【解析】** `since + 一般过去时从句` 是现在完成时的标志，主句需用现在完成时 `has worked`。

**59. — ____ you ever ____ the Universal Beijing Resort?**
**— Yes, I went there with my friends last summer.**
A. Do; visit  B. Have; visited  C. Did; visit  D. Will; visit
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 现在完成时
* **【解析】** 句中含有标志词 `ever`（曾经），询问过往经历用现在完成时 `Have... visited`。

**60. I ____ the book *Red Star Over China* when my friend knocked at the door.**
A. read  B. am reading  C. was reading  D. have read
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 过去进行时
* **【解析】** `when my friend knocked at the door` 表示过去某个瞬间发生的动作，主句表示该瞬间正在进行的背景动作，用过去进行时 `was reading`。

**61. Look at the dark clouds in the sky! I think it ____ soon.**
A. rains  B. rained  C. was raining  D. is going to rain
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 一般将来时
* **【解析】** 根据当前迹象（天上的乌云）以及时间状语 `soon` 预测即将发生的事，用 `is going to rain`。

**62. My sister ____ three English novels since the winter vacation started.**
A. reads  B. read  C. has read  D. is reading
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 现在完成时
* **【解析】** `since the winter vacation started` 为 `since` 引导的时间状语从句，主句表示从过去持续到现在的动作结果，用现在完成时 `has read`。

**63. Listen! Some girls ____ an English song in the next classroom.**
A. sing  B. sang  C. are singing  D. have sung
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 现在进行时
* **【解析】** 句首的 `Listen!`（听！）提示动作此刻正在进行，用现在进行时 `are singing`。

**64. Our geography teacher told us that the Earth ____ around the Sun.**
A. moves  B. moved  C. is moving  D. will move
* **【答案】** A
* **【考点】** 一般现在时（客观真理）
* **【解析】** 尽管主句动词 `told` 是过去时，但从句“地球绕着太阳转”属于客观真理和科学事实，永远使用一般现在时 `moves`。

**65. — Where is your brother, Jim?**
**— He ____ his bike in the yard at the moment.**
A. fixes  B. fixed  C. is fixing  D. has fixed
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 现在进行时
* **【解析】** 问句询问“你哥哥现在在哪”，且答语有标志词 `at the moment`（此刻，现在），应用现在进行时 `is fixing`。

**66. Great changes ____ in my hometown in the past ten years.**
A. take place  B. took place  C. will take place  D. have taken place
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 现在完成时
* **【解析】** `in the past ten years`（在过去的十年里）是典型的现在完成时标志词，故填 `have taken place`。

**67. — Peter, you look so tired today. What's wrong?**
**— I ____ up late last night to prepare for the speech.**
A. stay  B. stayed  C. am staying  D. will stay
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 一般过去时
* **【解析】** 句中有明确过去时间状语 `last night`（昨晚），应用一般过去时 `stayed`。

**68. Don't worry. I ____ you as soon as I get the ticket tomorrow.**
A. tell  B. told  C. will tell  D. have told
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 一般将来时（主将从现）
* **【解析】** `as soon as` 引导时间状语从句遵循“主将从现”原则，从句 `I get...` 为一般现在时表将来，主句用一般将来时 `will tell`。

**69. While my mother ____ dinner yesterday evening, my father was cleaning the living room.**
A. cooks  B. cooked  C. is cooking  D. was cooking
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 过去进行时
* **【解析】** `While` 引导时间状语从句，强调过去同一时间段内两个长动作同时正在进行，主句是 `was cleaning`，从句也用过去进行时 `was cooking`。

**70. — Have you finished your homework yet, Sam?**
**— Yes. I ____ it half an hour ago.**
A. finish  B. finished  C. have finished  D. will finish
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 一般过去时与现在完成时的辨析
* **【解析】** 虽然问句用了现在完成时，但答语中有非常明确的过去时间状语 `half an hour ago`（半小时前）。凡是有具体过去时间状语（`...ago, yesterday, last...`）的句子，必须用一般过去时 `finished`。

**71. Miss Wang ____ English in our school for twelve years. All the students love her.**
A. teaches  B. taught  C. is teaching  D. has taught
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 现在完成时
* **【解析】** 时间状语 `for twelve years`（长达十二年）且后句 `love` 为一般现在时，说明她现在依然在学校教书，动作从过去持续到现在，用现在完成时 `has taught`。

**72. Look! The children ____ happily on the snow. Let's go and join them.**
A. play  B. played  C. are playing  D. have played
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 现在进行时
* **【解析】** 句首 `Look!`（看！）和后句“我们去加入他们吧”说明孩子们此刻正在雪地上玩耍，用现在进行时 `are playing`。

**73. My uncle ____ to New York on business next Monday.**
A. flies  B. flew  C. will fly  D. has flown
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 一般将来时
* **【解析】** 时间状语 `next Monday`（下周一）表示将来时间，用一般将来时 `will fly`。

**74. — Why didn't you see the red light just now?**
**— Sorry, I ____ at my phone at that moment.**
A. look  B. looked  C. am looking  D. was looking
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 过去进行时
* **【解析】** 问句问刚才为什么没看见红灯，答语 `at that moment`（在刚才那个时刻）表示过去某个具体时刻正在进行的动作，用过去进行时 `was looking`。

**75. China ____ many communications satellites into space so far.**
A. sends  B. sent  C. will send  D. has sent
* **【答案】** D

## 模块八：被动语态（第77～88题）
**【中考命题规律】** 固定位于第11题。北京中考仅考查**三种时态的被动语态**：
1. 一般现在时被动：`am / is / are + done`
2. 一般过去时被动：`was / were + done`
3. 一般将来时被动：`will be + done`
**秒杀两步法**：第一步，看主语是“动作的执行者”还是“承受者”（承受者必选 `be + done`）；第二步，圈出时间状语，定准时态。

**77. Today, AI technology ____ widely in many fields, such as education and medicine.**
A. uses  B. is used  C. used  D. was used
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 一般现在时的被动语态
* **【解析】** 主语 `AI technology` 是被使用的，排除A、C；时间状语 `Today`（如今）表示现在，用一般现在时的被动语态 `is used`。

**78. The 24th Winter Olympic Games ____ successfully in Beijing and Zhangjiakou in 2022.**
A. hold  B. held  C. are held  D. were held
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 一般过去时的被动语态
* **【解析】** 主语“第24届冬奥会”是被举办的；时间状语 `in 2022` 为过去时间，且主语 `Games` 为复数，故用 `were held`。

**79. A new sports center ____ in our school next year. We are all looking forward to it.**
A. builds  B. will build  C. is built  D. will be built
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 一般将来时的被动语态
* **【解析】** 主语 `A new sports center` 是被建造的；时间状语 `next year` 表将来，故用一般将来时的被动语态 `will be built`。

**80. Many trees and flowers ____ along the streets in our city every spring.**
A. plant  B. planted  C. are planted  D. were planted
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 一般现在时的被动语态
* **【解析】** 主语 `Many trees and flowers` 是被种植的；时间状语 `every spring` 表经常性动作，用一般现在时的被动语态 `are planted`。

**81. — Mom, where is my raincoat? I can't find it anywhere.**
**— Oh, it ____ to your cousin yesterday because it was raining hard when he left.**
A. lends  B. lent  C. is lent  D. was lent
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 一般过去时的被动语态
* **【解析】** 主语 `it`（雨衣）是被借出的；时间状语 `yesterday` 表过去，用一般过去时的被动语态 `was lent`。

**82. The classic novel *Journey to the West* ____ by Wu Cheng'en in the Ming Dynasty.**
A. writes  B. wrote  C. is written  D. was written
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 一般过去时的被动语态
* **【解析】** 主语《西游记》是被写的，且有标志词 `by Wu Cheng'en`；时间状语 `in the Ming Dynasty`（在明朝）表过去，用 `was written`。

**83. Chinese ____ by more and more people around the world nowadays.**
A. speaks  B. spoke  C. is spoken  D. was spoken
* **【答案】** C
* **【考点】** 一般现在时的被动语态
* **【解析】** 主语 `Chinese`（汉语）是被讲的；时间状语 `nowadays`（现今）表现在，用一般现在时的被动语态 `is spoken`。

**84. A speech on space science ____ in our school hall tomorrow afternoon.**
A. gives  B. will give  C. is given  D. will be given
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 一般将来时的被动语态
* **【解析】** 主语 `A speech`（一场演讲）是被做的；时间状语 `tomorrow afternoon` 表将来，用一般将来时的被动语态 `will be given`。

**85. These beautiful clay pieces ____ by the students in the art club last week.**
A. make  B. made  C. are made  D. were made
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 一般过去时的被动语态
* **【解析】** 主语 `These beautiful clay pieces`（这些漂亮的陶艺作品，复数）是被制作的；时间状语 `last week` 表过去，用 `were made`。

**86. Passengers ____ to show their ID cards before they get on the high-speed train.**
A. ask  B. asked  C. are asked  D. were asked
* **【答案】** C

* **【解析】** 主语 `Passengers`（乘客）是被要求出示身份证的；从句 `before they get on...` 为一般现在时，陈述日常规定，用 `are asked`。

**87. Look at the old bridge over there! It ____ more than three hundred years ago.**
A. builds  B. built  C. is built  D. was built
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 一般过去时的被动语态
* **【解析】** 主语 `It`（老桥）是被建造的；时间状语 `more than three hundred years ago`（三百多年前）表过去，用 `was built`。

**88. Don't worry about the lost books. They ____ to the library as soon as we find them.**

* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 一般将来时的被动语态
* **【解析】** 主语 `They`（丢失的书）是被归还的；`as soon as` 引导的时间状语从句遵循“主将从现”原则，主句需用一般将来时的被动语态 `will be returned`。

## 模块九：宾语从句（第89～100题）
**【中考命题规律】** 永远位于单选压轴第12题！解题三步口诀：**“一排疑问语序，二定主从时态，三看答语选连词”**。
1. **第一步（看语序）**：宾语从句必须是**陈述句语序**（连接词 + 主语 + 谓语动词），直接排除助动词/情态动词/be动词提到主语前面的疑问语序选项（瞬間排除2个错误选项）。
2. **第二步（看时态）**：观察句中时间状语（如 `yesterday, next week`）或主句谓语时态。注意：`Could you please tell me...` 是委婉语气，不是过去时！
3. **第三步（看连接词）**：根据答语的内容决定用 `what / where / when / why / how / who`。

**89. — Could you please tell me ____?**
**— Next Friday afternoon.**
A. when will we have the school sports meeting
B. when we will have the school sports meeting
C. where will we have the school sports meeting
D. where we will have the school sports meeting
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 宾语从句（语序 + 连接词）
* **【解析】** 第一步：排除疑问语序 A 和 C；第二步：答语 `Next Friday afternoon`（下周五下午）回答的是时间，故用连接词 `when`，选B。

**90. — Do you know ____ the Beijing Library in Tongzhou?**
**— Yes. You can take Subway Line 1 and then take a bus.**
A. how I can get to
B. how can I get to
C. when I can get to
D. when can I get to
* **【答案】** A
* **【考点】** 宾语从句（语序 + 连接词）
* **【解析】** 第一步：排除疑问语序 B 和 D；第二步：答语“坐地铁1号线然后转公交车”回答的是交通方式，用连接词 `how`，选A。

**91. — Excuse me, I'd like to know ____ yesterday.**
**— Because the traffic was too heavy.**
A. why are you late
B. why you are late
C. why were you late
D. why you were late
* **【答案】** D
* **【考点】** 宾语从句（语序 + 时态）
* **【解析】** 第一步：排除疑问语序 A 和 C；第二步：句末有过去时间状语 `yesterday`，从句必须用一般过去时，选D。

**92. — Jack, do you remember ____ when we visited the Palace Museum last month?**
**— Of course! We took it in front of the Hall of Supreme Harmony (太和殿).**
A. where did we take this photo
B. where we took this photo
C. when did we take this photo
D. when we took this photo
* **【答案】** B
* **【考点】** 宾语从句（语序 + 连接词）
* **【解析】** 第一步：排除疑问语序 A 和 C；第二步：答语 `in front of the Hall of Supreme Harmony`（在太和殿前）表地点，用连接词 `where`，选B。

**93. — Have you decided ____ for your mother's birthday?**
**— Yes, I'm going to make a birthday cake for her by myself.**
A. what will you buy
B. what you will buy
C. what did you buy
D. what you bought

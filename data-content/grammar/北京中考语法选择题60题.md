# 北京中考英语语法选择题题库（60题·逐题解析）

> **适用对象**：初三学生、北京中考英语复习  
> **题型**：四选一单项选择  
> **说明**：本题库为原创模拟练习，并非历年真题汇编。参考2025年北京中考英语单项填空的语境化命题特点编写；本题库题数与难度分布不代表官方考试要求。

## 使用说明

- 共 **60题**，分11个专题；每道题均包含四个选项、正确答案、语法考点和中文解析。
- 建议先遮住答案独立完成，再阅读解析并整理错题。
- 难度标注仅用于自主练习，不表示官方难度等级。

## 一、代词与限定词

### 第 1 题｜基础

My brother enjoys reading science books. ______ often spends his weekends in the library.

A. He  
B. She  
C. It  
D. They

**答案：A**

**考点：** 人称代词主格

**解析：** My brother 指男性单数，空格作主语，故用 He。

### 第 2 题｜基础

This is not my dictionary. I think it is ______.

A. her  
B. hers  
C. she  
D. herself

**答案：B**

**考点：** 名词性物主代词

**解析：** 空格后没有名词，需用名词性物主代词 hers，相当于 her dictionary。

### 第 3 题｜提升

The students made the model plane by ______.

A. they  
B. them  
C. their  
D. themselves

**答案：D**

**考点：** 反身代词

**解析：** by oneself 意为“独立地、靠自己”，主语为复数 students，故用 themselves。

### 第 4 题｜提升

There are two notebooks on the desk. One is mine, and ______ is Lucy’s.

A. another  
B. other  
C. the other  
D. others

**答案：C**

**考点：** 不定代词

**解析：** 两者中的“另一个”用 the other；another 常指三者及以上中的另一个。

### 第 5 题｜易错

—Which of these two T-shirts would you like? —______ is fine. I like both colours.

A. Either  
B. Neither  
C. All  
D. None

**答案：A**

**考点：** 不定代词

**解析：** 两件T恤中的任意一件都可以，用 either；neither 表示两者都不。

## 二、名词、冠词与数词

### 第 6 题｜基础

My uncle is ______ engineer. He works on new energy projects.

A. a  
B. an  
C. the  
D. /

**答案：B**

**考点：** 不定冠词

**解析：** engineer 以元音音素开头，表示职业且首次提及，用 an。

### 第 7 题｜基础

The children are looking at ______ moon through the window.

A. a  
B. an  
C. the  
D. /

**答案：C**

**考点：** 定冠词

**解析：** moon 表示通常意义上的月亮，为独一无二的事物，常用 the。

### 第 8 题｜基础

There are many ______ in the school library.

A. book  
B. books  
C. book’s  
D. books’

**答案：B**

**考点：** 可数名词复数

**解析：** many 修饰可数名词复数，故用 books。

### 第 9 题｜提升

We held a party for my grandfather’s ______ birthday yesterday.

A. seventy  
B. seventieth  
C. the seventy  
D. seventies

**答案：B**

**考点：** 序数词

**解析：** 表示“第七十个生日”要用序数词 seventieth，前面已有物主代词 grandfather’s，不再加 the。

### 第 10 题｜易错

—What a beautiful painting! Who made it? —It’s one of the ______ works.

A. student  
B. students  
C. students’  
D. student’s

**答案：C**

**考点：** 复数名词所有格

**解析：** one of 后接复数名词，works 属于多个学生，故用 students’。

## 三、介词与固定搭配

### 第 11 题｜基础

The school sports meeting will be held ______ Friday morning.

A. in  
B. on  
C. at  
D. to

**答案：B**

**考点：** 时间介词

**解析：** 具体到某天的早晨用 on，如 on Friday morning。

### 第 12 题｜基础

My sister has been interested ______ space science since she was little.

A. on  
B. at  
C. in  
D. for

**答案：C**

**考点：** 形容词与介词搭配

**解析：** be interested in 是固定搭配，意为“对……感兴趣”。

### 第 13 题｜基础

The new bridge goes ______ the river and connects the two villages.

A. across  
B. through  
C. along  
D. between

**答案：A**

**考点：** 方位介词

**解析：** across 表示从表面横跨；through 表示从内部穿过。桥横跨河流用 across。

### 第 14 题｜提升

We have studied English ______ nearly six years.

A. since  
B. for  
C. at  
D. until

**答案：B**

**考点：** 时间介词与现在完成时

**解析：** for 后接时间段 nearly six years；since 后通常接时间点或从句。

### 第 15 题｜基础

—How did you get to the museum? —I went there ______ underground.

A. by  
B. with  
C. in  
D. on

**答案：A**

**考点：** 交通方式

**解析：** by underground 意为“乘地铁”，by 后接交通工具名词时通常不加冠词。

## 四、形容词与副词

### 第 16 题｜基础

This maths problem is ______ than the one we did yesterday.

A. difficult  
B. more difficult  
C. most difficult  
D. the most difficult

**答案：B**

**考点：** 形容词比较级

**解析：** than 是比较级标志，多音节形容词 difficult 用 more difficult。

### 第 17 题｜基础

Of all the students in the team, Amy runs ______.

A. fast  
B. faster  
C. fastest  
D. the faster

**答案：C**

**考点：** 副词最高级

**解析：** of all 表示三者及以上的比较，应使用最高级 fastest；副词最高级前的 the 可省略。

### 第 18 题｜提升

Please speak ______ so that everyone can hear you clearly.

A. loud  
B. louder  
C. more loudly  
D. most loudly

**答案：C**

**考点：** 副词比较级

**解析：** 语境含有“比现在更响亮”的要求，speak 用副词修饰，more loudly 表示“更大声地”。

### 第 19 题｜基础

The boy felt ______ when he heard the good news.

A. excite  
B. exciting  
C. excited  
D. excitement

**答案：C**

**考点：** -ed/-ing 形容词

**解析：** 描述人的感受用 excited；exciting 通常描述令人兴奋的事物。

### 第 20 题｜易错

The weather today is ______ for us to have a picnic outside.

A. enough warm  
B. warm enough  
C. too warmly  
D. enough warmly

**答案：B**

**考点：** enough 的位置

**解析：** 形容词 + enough 表示“足够……”，warm enough 正确；warmly 为副词。

## 五、情态动词与祈使句

### 第 21 题｜基础

—______ I borrow your pen, please? —Of course. Here you are.

A. May  
B. Must  
C. Should  
D. Need

**答案：A**

**考点：** 请求许可

**解析：** May I ...? 是礼貌地请求许可的表达。

### 第 22 题｜基础

You ______ take photos here. Look at the “No Photos” sign.

A. needn’t  
B. mustn’t  
C. may  
D. could

**答案：B**

**考点：** 情态动词表禁止

**解析：** mustn’t 表示“不准、禁止”，与 No Photos 标识一致。

### 第 23 题｜易错

—Must I finish the report tonight? —No, you ______. You can do it tomorrow.

A. mustn’t  
B. can’t  
C. needn’t  
D. shouldn’t

**答案：C**

**考点：** must 提问的否定回答

**解析：** Must I ...? 的否定回答可用 No, you needn’t / don’t have to，表示“不必”。

### 第 24 题｜基础

______ careful when you cross the busy street.

A. Be  
B. Being  
C. To be  
D. Been

**答案：A**

**考点：** 肯定祈使句

**解析：** 祈使句以动词原形开头；be careful 意为“小心”。

### 第 25 题｜提升

—Whose bag is this? —It ______ be Mike’s. His name is on it.

A. can’t  
B. must  
C. need  
D. shouldn’t

**答案：B**

**考点：** 情态动词表推测

**解析：** 根据包上有 Mike 的名字可作较有把握的肯定推测，用 must。

## 六、动词时态

### 第 26 题｜基础

My father ______ to work by bike every day.

A. goes  
B. went  
C. is going  
D. has gone

**答案：A**

**考点：** 一般现在时

**解析：** every day 表示习惯性动作；主语 my father 为第三人称单数，故用 goes。

### 第 27 题｜基础

We ______ a wonderful science show at the museum last Saturday.

A. watch  
B. watched  
C. are watching  
D. have watched

**答案：B**

**考点：** 一般过去时

**解析：** last Saturday 是过去时间状语，谓语用一般过去时 watched。

### 第 28 题｜基础

—Where is your mother? —She ______ dinner in the kitchen.

A. cooks  
B. cooked  
C. is cooking  
D. has cooked

**答案：C**

**考点：** 现在进行时

**解析：** 问句询问此刻所在位置，答句描述正在进行的动作，用 is cooking。

### 第 29 题｜提升

I ______ you as soon as I arrive at the station tomorrow.

A. call  
B. called  
C. will call  
D. have called

**答案：C**

**考点：** 一般将来时与时间状语从句

**解析：** 主句表示将来动作，用 will call；as soon as 引导的时间状语从句用一般现在时表将来。

### 第 30 题｜基础

When I entered the classroom, my classmates ______ about the school trip.

A. talk  
B. are talking  
C. were talking  
D. have talked

**答案：C**

**考点：** 过去进行时

**解析：** entered 是过去某个时间点，表示当时正在讨论，用 were talking。

### 第 31 题｜提升

I ______ this book twice, so I know the story quite well.

A. read  
B. am reading  
C. have read  
D. will read

**答案：C**

**考点：** 现在完成时

**解析：** twice 表示截至目前已有两次阅读经历，并对现在产生影响，使用 have read。

### 第 32 题｜基础

My parents ______ in Beijing since 2012.

A. live  
B. lived  
C. have lived  
D. will live

**答案：C**

**考点：** 现在完成时与 since

**解析：** since 2012 表示从过去持续至今，谓语用 have lived。

### 第 33 题｜提升

Look at those dark clouds! It ______ soon.

A. rains  
B. rained  
C. is going to rain  
D. has rained

**答案：C**

**考点：** be going to 表预测

**解析：** 根据乌云这一现有迹象推测即将下雨，使用 is going to rain。

### 第 34 题｜易错

By the time the movie started, we ______ our seats.

A. find  
B. found  
C. had found  
D. will find

**答案：C**

**考点：** 过去完成时

**解析：** 找座位发生在电影开始之前，两个过去动作有先后，较早动作用 had found。

### 第 35 题｜易错

—Have you seen Alice? —Yes. She ______ to the reading room a few minutes ago.

A. goes  
B. went  
C. has gone  
D. was going

**答案：B**

**考点：** 一般过去时与时间状语

**解析：** a few minutes ago 指明确过去时间，需用 went，不能用现在完成时。

## 七、被动语态

### 第 36 题｜基础

More trees ______ in our neighbourhood every spring.

A. plant  
B. planted  
C. are planted  
D. were planted

**答案：C**

**考点：** 一般现在时被动语态

**解析：** trees 与 plant 构成被动关系，every spring 指经常性动作，用 are planted。

### 第 37 题｜基础

The new sports centre ______ last year.

A. builds  
B. built  
C. is built  
D. was built

**答案：D**

**考点：** 一般过去时被动语态

**解析：** 主语 centre 是被建造的对象，last year 指过去，故用 was built。

### 第 38 题｜提升

The classroom ______ after school tomorrow.

A. cleans  
B. cleaned  
C. will be cleaned  
D. has cleaned

**答案：C**

**考点：** 一般将来时被动语态

**解析：** tomorrow 表将来，classroom 承受 clean 动作，用 will be cleaned。

### 第 39 题｜基础

English ______ in many countries around the world.

A. speaks  
B. is spoken  
C. spoke  
D. was speaking

**答案：B**

**考点：** 一般现在时被动语态

**解析：** 语言被人使用，描述客观事实，用 is spoken。

### 第 40 题｜易错

—Why is the door locked? —The room ______ for an important meeting now.

A. uses  
B. used  
C. is being used  
D. has used

**答案：C**

**考点：** 现在进行时被动语态

**解析：** now 表示此时正在使用，且 room 与 use 为被动关系，用 is being used。

## 八、非谓语动词

### 第 41 题｜基础

My teacher encourages us ______ questions in class.

A. ask  
B. asking  
C. to ask  
D. asked

**答案：C**

**考点：** 动词不定式作宾补

**解析：** encourage sb. to do sth. 表示“鼓励某人做某事”。

### 第 42 题｜基础

Jane enjoys ______ stories in English before bedtime.

A. read  
B. reading  
C. to read  
D. reads

**答案：B**

**考点：** 动名词作宾语

**解析：** enjoy doing sth. 为固定搭配，意为“喜欢做某事”。

### 第 43 题｜易错

We should stop ______ a rest after working for two hours.

A. have  
B. to have  
C. having  
D. had

**答案：B**

**考点：** stop to do 与 stop doing

**解析：** stop to do sth. 指停下当前事情去做另一件事；语境为停下来休息。

### 第 44 题｜提升

The box is too heavy for the little boy ______.

A. carry  
B. carrying  
C. to carry  
D. carried

**答案：C**

**考点：** too...to... 结构

**解析：** too + 形容词 + for sb. + to do 表示“对某人来说太……而不能做”。

### 第 45 题｜基础

______ healthy, we should exercise regularly and eat well.

A. Keep  
B. Keeping  
C. To keep  
D. Kept

**答案：C**

**考点：** 不定式作目的状语

**解析：** 句首 To keep healthy 表示“为了保持健康”，作目的状语。

## 九、连词与状语从句

### 第 46 题｜基础

I wanted to join the basketball team, ______ I needed more practice first.

A. and  
B. but  
C. or  
D. so

**答案：B**

**考点：** 并列连词表转折

**解析：** 想加入球队与首先需要更多训练形成转折，用 but。

### 第 47 题｜基础

It was raining heavily, ______ we stayed at home.

A. because  
B. so  
C. although  
D. unless

**答案：B**

**考点：** 并列连词表结果

**解析：** 前半句是原因，后半句为结果，用 so；because 不能和 so 按这一结构同时连用。

### 第 48 题｜基础

You will improve your English ______ you practise a little every day.

A. if  
B. unless  
C. though  
D. before

**答案：A**

**考点：** if 引导条件状语从句

**解析：** 每天练习是提高英语水平的条件，用 if，意为“如果”。

### 第 49 题｜提升

______ the task was difficult, the students finished it on time.

A. Because  
B. Although  
C. If  
D. Until

**答案：B**

**考点：** 让步状语从句

**解析：** “任务难”与“按时完成”构成让步关系，用 Although。

### 第 50 题｜易错

I’ll send you a message when I ______ home.

A. get  
B. got  
C. will get  
D. have got

**答案：A**

**考点：** 时间状语从句主将从现

**解析：** 主句 I’ll send 为一般将来时，when 引导的时间状语从句用一般现在时 get。

## 十、宾语从句与定语从句

### 第 51 题｜提升

—Could you tell me ______? —Sure. It’s on the second floor.

A. where is the art room  
B. where the art room is  
C. when does the art room open  
D. when the art room opens

**答案：B**

**考点：** 宾语从句语序及疑问词

**解析：** 宾语从句用陈述句语序，排除 A、C；答句回答地点，故选 B。

### 第 52 题｜提升

I wonder ______ the school trip will be held next week.

A. that  
B. if  
C. what  
D. wherever

**答案：B**

**考点：** if 引导宾语从句

**解析：** wonder 后可接 if 表示“是否”，语境询问下周能否举行研学活动。

### 第 53 题｜基础

—Do you know ______? —Yes, she bought it yesterday.

A. when did Lily buy the bike  
B. when Lily bought the bike  
C. where does Lily buy the bike  
D. where Lily buys the bike

**答案：B**

**考点：** 宾语从句陈述语序与时态

**解析：** 宾语从句要用陈述语序；回答 yesterday 表示购买时间在过去，故选 B。

### 第 54 题｜提升

The girl ______ won the speech competition is in Class Three.

A. which  
B. who  
C. where  
D. when

**答案：B**

**考点：** 关系代词 who

**解析：** 先行词 The girl 指人，关系代词在从句中作主语，用 who。

### 第 55 题｜提升

This is the book ______ helped me learn more about Chinese history.

A. who  
B. where  
C. that  
D. when

**答案：C**

**考点：** 关系代词 that

**解析：** 先行词 book 指物，关系代词在定语从句中作主语，可用 that。

## 十一、主谓一致与特殊句式

### 第 56 题｜基础

There ______ a science exhibition in our school next month.

A. is  
B. was  
C. will be  
D. has been

**答案：C**

**考点：** there be 的一般将来时

**解析：** next month 表将来，there be 句型的将来形式为 there will be。

### 第 57 题｜易错

Neither Tom nor his parents ______ at home now.

A. is  
B. are  
C. was  
D. be

**答案：B**

**考点：** 就近一致原则

**解析：** neither...nor... 连接主语时谓语与较近的主语 his parents 保持一致，用 are。

### 第 58 题｜提升

Reading books ______ a good way to relax after school.

A. am  
B. is  
C. are  
D. be

**答案：B**

**考点：** 动名词短语作主语

**解析：** Reading books 作主语表示一件活动，谓语动词用单数 is。

### 第 59 题｜易错

______ useful advice our teacher gave us yesterday!

A. What  
B. What a  
C. How  
D. How a

**答案：A**

**考点：** 感叹句

**解析：** advice 是不可数名词，结构为 What + 形容词 + 不可数名词 + 主语 + 谓语。

### 第 60 题｜基础

The question is so easy that ______ can answer it.

A. nobody  
B. anybody  
C. nothing  
D. anywhere

**答案：B**

**考点：** 不定代词与结果状语从句

**解析：** so...that... 表示“如此……以至于……”，题目很容易，任何人都能答，用 anybody。
